Free Launch Access

AirPong Motion is free at launch. The launch version has no subscriptions and no advertising.

Optional cosmetic purchases may be added later. If they are added, they will be optional and billing will be handled by the App Store.

Controls

The intended experience uses compatible AirPods with motion support. Availability depends on your device, headset, iOS version, permissions, and selected audio output.

Tilt fallback is available when compatible AirPods are not connected.

Leaderboards & Multiplayer

Game Center leaderboards, achievements, and Game Center multiplayer are provided by Apple. Availability and identity handling depend on your device, Apple ID, Game Center settings, and Apple's platform rules.

Nearby multiplayer depends on local network access, compatible app versions, and nearby device connectivity.
