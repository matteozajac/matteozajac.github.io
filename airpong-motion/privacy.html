Privacy Policy

How AirPong Motion handles local play, motion controls, and multiplayer.

Overview

Last updated: June 2026

AirPong Motion is designed around local arcade play. The app does not collect personal data for the developer, does not track you, and does not use advertising tracking domains.

Local Data

AirPong Motion stores game settings, control preferences, calibration choices, and local gameplay state on your device so the app can remember how you like to play.

You can remove local game data by deleting the app from your device.

Motion Controls

AirPong Motion uses compatible AirPods motion data and device tilt data to move the paddle during gameplay. Motion readings are processed on your device for controls and are not collected by the developer.

Motion access can be managed in iOS Settings.

Game Center & Multiplayer

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center identity, leaderboards, achievements, matchmaking, and related platform features under Apple's own privacy practices.

Nearby multiplayer uses the local network to discover and connect to nearby players. During multiplayer matches, gameplay state and paddle input may be exchanged with the other player so the match can run.

Questions & Requests

If you have privacy questions or need help understanding how your data is handled, contact email.
