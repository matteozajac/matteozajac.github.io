Free Access and Purchases

Journey levels 1–5 are free to replay. Free Play, Daily and Weekly Challenges share three free starts per local day; restarting these boards uses a start. One non-consumable Full Game purchase unlocks all 30 Journey levels and removes the daily start limit. There are no subscriptions or advertisements.

The App Store displays the applicable local price before you confirm. Apple processes payments, authorizations and refunds. Use Restore Purchases in Settings or Restore on the offer to recover eligible purchases associated with the same Apple Account. A refunded or revoked purchase may remove paid access.

Progress and Online Services

Core gameplay works offline, while purchases, restoration and Game Center depend on Apple and RevenueCat services. Journey progress is stored on your device and is not synchronized by the app across devices. Removing the app removes its local data. Restoring a purchase restores paid access, not game progress.

Rankings and achievements depend on Game Center availability. Pausing or leaving a shared challenge makes the current attempt unranked. Features and compatibility may change through app updates.

Safe Use and Support

On Apple Vision Pro, follow Apple Vision Pro safety guidance, keep your physical surroundings clear and take breaks when needed.
