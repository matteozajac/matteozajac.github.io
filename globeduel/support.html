How to play

Open a round, inspect the Look Around panorama, tap Choose location, place a pin, and confirm before the 75-second timer ends. Closer guesses score more, up to 5,000 points per round and 25,000 for a five-round game.

Use Practice for a solo game, the home postcards for ten-level journeys, and the daily card for the shared daily challenge. Duel and Invite friend use Game Center. SharePlay starts from FaceTime.

Panoramas and network

Look Around needs a network connection and current Apple Maps coverage. If a scene cannot load, Practice skips to another catalog place. Journey and daily routes retry the same stop instead of substituting a random city. Coverage can change over time; a saved coordinate is not a permanent guarantee of imagery.

Game Center and progress

Solo practice and journeys store progress on this iPhone. Deleting the app removes that local progress. Daily and weekly leaderboards, friend challenges, and live duels need Game Center and an internet connection. If rankings do not appear, confirm Game Center is enabled in iOS Settings and that the device is online.

Contact
