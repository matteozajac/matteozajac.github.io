Access and price

GlobeDuel is free to download. This version has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising. Features that use Apple Maps, Game Center, or SharePlay depend on Apple’s services, your Apple Account, network access, and regional availability.

Gameplay

Rounds use Apple Look Around imagery. Scores are based on the distance between your pin and the authored seed coordinate for that round, not a later street you may have walked to in the panorama. Missing or delayed imagery can skip a place or pause a round. Daily and weekly challenges are shared by calendar date; journey progress is stored only on the device that played it.

Live duels need a second player through Game Center or SharePlay. Casual duel results are not an official ranked rating. Do not cheat, tamper with the app, interfere with other players, or use it in a way that violates law or the rights of others.
