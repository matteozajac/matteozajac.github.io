Free access and the Core Library

The first three lessons are available without a purchase. The Core Library is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks access to the remaining lessons in the current 24-lesson library. Lessons follow a sequence: finish the current lesson to continue to the next one. A purchase does not skip that learning sequence. It is not an auto-renewing subscription. “Lifetime” access refers to this purchased entitlement, subject to Apple’s terms and continued app compatibility; it is not a promise of a particular period of updates or new content.

The current localized price appears in the app before purchase. Apple handles payment and purchase terms. Eligible purchases can be restored using the same Apple Account through Settings → Restore purchases. A refund or revoked transaction can remove paid access. Contact Apple through Report a Problem for refund requests.

Your saved work

Sessions and prep cards are stored locally on your iPhone and are excluded from backups. They are not synced to a Second Take account or iCloud. App deletion, device loss, or deletion under your retention choices can remove them. Restoring purchases restores eligible library access, not history. If you want a separate copy of a prep card, use the app’s copy or share action and choose a suitable destination.

Compatibility and external resources

The app requires iPhone with iOS 26 or later. English is the current supported language. Guided scenarios and learning guides are bundled with the app; purchases, restoration, and external reading links require an internet connection.
