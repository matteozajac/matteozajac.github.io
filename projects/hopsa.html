Hopsa

An illustrated arcade climb through Polish places, stories, and folklore.

About

Hopsa turns an always-upward arcade climb into a 50-chapter journey across Poland. The hero bounces automatically; you steer every landing through changing landscapes, cities, legends, literature, and a final ascent to the Summit.

The game is built for quick runs and long-term score chasing, with no advertising and no account required. Core gameplay works offline in Polish or English on iPhone and iPad.

PlatformiPhone and iPad
ModelFree, optional one-time cosmetics
ServicesGame Center and RevenueCat
LanguagesEnglish and Polish

Made to Keep You Climbing

Seven platform types, hazards, collectible pierogi, three power-ups, achievements, and 49 distinct illustrated settings keep each ascent changing. Reach the 36-landing Summit and the climb continues beyond it for your next high score.

Touch, tilt, on-screen buttons, keyboard, and game controllers are supported. Five optional historical character designs can be purchased individually or unlocked together with the Hopsa Character Pass; every purchase is cosmetic and permanent.
