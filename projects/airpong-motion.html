AirPong Motion

A head-motion arcade paddle game for compatible AirPods.

About

AirPong Motion is a fast arcade paddle game built around head movement. Put on compatible AirPods, center your head, and guide the paddle by moving naturally as each rally speeds up.

The game is designed for short score-chasing runs, with tilt fallback available when compatible AirPods are not connected.

Platform iOS
Model Free at launch
Services Game Center and local network multiplayer
Status App Store launch setup in progress

Game Loop

Return the ball, chase longer rallies, and push your best score one hit higher. Game Center leaderboards and achievements give AirPods runs a clear score target.

AirPong Motion also supports nearby multiplayer and Game Center multiplayer for quick head-to-head matches.
