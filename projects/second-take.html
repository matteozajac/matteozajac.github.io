Second Take

Build confidence in everyday team conversations, one lesson at a time.

A little practice before the real conversation

Give useful feedback. Set a clear boundary. Delegate with confidence. Second Take helps you find your words for the everyday conversations that shape a team.

Follow a path of 40 short lessons. Start with a familiar workplace situation, try a reply, and see how the conversation develops. Each lesson gives you one idea to understand and a practical way to try it.

PlatformiPhone and iPad · iOS/iPadOS 26 or later
Library40 guided lessons in English
Access3 free lessons, optional one-time unlock
AvailabilityPreparing for the first App Store release

Find a better second take

Work through six conversational decisions in each lesson and get feedback on your choices. Rewind a moment to try another approach, compare your takes, and turn what you learned into a prep card in your own words.

Lessons cover feedback, boundaries, delegation, priorities, disagreement, and other everyday team situations. Search for a topic or save a lesson in Bookmarks to return to it. Finish the current lesson to continue along the path.

Your practice stays yours

No Second Take account is required. Rehearsals, prep cards, reflections, and your learning progress stay on your device. Guided practice works offline and does not require Apple Intelligence.

Usage analytics and crash diagnostics run automatically, with no in-app permission prompt or switch. Private rehearsal text and prep-card content are not sent to these services. Purchases are handled by Apple and verified with RevenueCat. The Core Library is a one-time purchase, with no subscription; the app shows the current price before you buy.
