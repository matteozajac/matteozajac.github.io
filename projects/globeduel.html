GlobeDuel

See a real street. Drop a pin. Duel a friend.

About

GlobeDuel is a geography guessing game for iPhone. Each round opens a real Apple Look Around panorama. You read the street, place one pin on the map, and score by distance — up to 5,000 points a round.

Duel a friend through Game Center or SharePlay, play a five-round practice game on your own, take the shared daily challenge, or walk five ten-level journeys. No GlobeDuel account is required.

PlatformiPhone · iOS 27
ModelFree, no in-app purchases
ServicesLook Around, Game Center, SharePlay
LanguagesEnglish and Polish

Play on real streets

Panoramas come from Apple Maps. You can look around nearby streets during the 75-second round, then confirm a pin before the clock runs out. Practice, journeys, and the daily game work without signing in. Live duels and leaderboards use Game Center when you choose to connect.

Progress stays on this iPhone. The app has no ads, no tracking, and no purchase screen in this first release.
