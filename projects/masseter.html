Masseter

Jaw clenching awareness, one check-in at a time.

About

Masseter is an iOS app designed to help you become aware of jaw clenching and teeth grinding (bruxism) habits throughout the day. By logging quick check-ins — tense or relaxed — you build a clear picture of your patterns and can take steps to reduce tension.

The app includes an Apple Watch companion for frictionless logging, home screen widgets for instant check-ins, smart notifications to prompt regular self-checks, and iCloud sync to keep your data consistent across devices.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Health history and iCloud

Masseter does not require a Masseter account. Your check-ins and health history are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, they also sync through Apple CloudKit to your private iCloud account. We do not send that health history to RevenueCat, Firebase or Sentry. Reports and exports you choose to share remain under your control.

Purchases and Apple Ads measurement

RevenueCat validates purchases, restores access and keeps purchase history. Apple's standard AdServices attribution can provide acquisition information, including campaign, ad group, keyword and country when available. RevenueCat associates that information with a pseudonymous customer record so we can measure purchases and revenue attributed to Apple Ads. This integration adds no ATT prompt or IDFA collection. Your health history is not used for this measurement.

Usage analytics and diagnostics

Firebase receives usage events, and Sentry receives sanitized crash and error diagnostics. These services can receive an app-generated pseudonymous ID and technical app/device information. Where usage analytics is enabled, Firebase also receives verified purchase analytics. Health values and free-text health entries are excluded.

The Firebase usage-analytics and Sentry diagnostics switches are on by default and can be turned off separately in Settings. They do not disable RevenueCat purchase management or Apple Ads acquisition measurement.

Advertising and data retention

Masseter does not display advertisements or sell your health history. We do not use your health history to target advertisements. The Apple Ads connection described above measures acquisition and purchases.

Deleting the app removes its local application data; it does not automatically delete private iCloud data, Apple Health data, Apple's purchase records, or records held by our service providers. Provider data is subject to their configured retention and deletion practices. No fixed retention period is promised here. For questions or a data deletion request, contact matteo.zajac@gmail.com.

Masseter is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

What is jaw clenching?

Jaw clenching (bruxism) is the involuntary tightening of your jaw muscles, often caused by stress. Over time it can lead to headaches, jaw pain, and dental issues. Masseter helps you notice these moments so you can consciously relax.

How do check-ins work?

When a notification arrives — or anytime you remember — tap "Tense" or "Relaxed" to log your current jaw state. Over time, the app reveals your patterns through daily summaries and trends.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. All data is stored locally on your device. If you opt in to iCloud sync, data is stored in your private iCloud container — only you can access it.
