Privacy Policy

How Hopsa handles progress, purchases, motion controls, and platform services.

Overview

Effective August 29, 2026

Hopsa requires no account, shows no ads, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal data.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your best and most recent scores, collected-pierogi total, run count, language, control settings, tutorial progress, achievements, and selected character are stored locally on your device. Deleting Hopsa removes this local data.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Optional one-time purchases are processed by Apple. Hopsa uses RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts, restore access, prevent fraud, and understand aggregate purchase performance. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase history. Hopsa does not send RevenueCat your name, email address, or a custom account identifier.

Purchase information is used for app functionality and analytics. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Learn more in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Motion Controls

If you enable tilt controls, Hopsa reads accelerometer data only while you play. Motion samples steer the character in real time; Hopsa does not store them or send them to the developer.

Game Center

If you enable Game Center, Hopsa sends your best score and completed achievements to Apple and displays information supplied by Game Center. Game Center is optional, and you can play without it.

Permissions and Support Website

Hopsa may request Motion & Fitness access for tilt controls. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or advertising identifier.

This support website is delivered by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary network information needed to serve a webpage. Website hosting is separate from the Hopsa app.

Changes and Questions

Material changes will be published here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact email.
