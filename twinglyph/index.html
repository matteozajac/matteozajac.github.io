TwinGlyph

A calm 3D spatial puzzle for Apple Vision Pro. Match glyphs in a lantern-lit garden.

The Jade Path

Match colorful raised glyphs, clear the board, and settle into the rhythm of each puzzle. Follow a 30-level Journey, choose your own Free Play board, or try the shared Daily and Weekly Challenges.

Play inside Jade Sanctuary, a garden with shifting daylight, lanterns and a peaceful pond. Look at a glyph and pinch to select it, then choose its pair the same way. Settings can turn the sanctuary off for passthrough play.

Play your way

Journey levels 1–5 are free to replay. Free Play, Daily and Weekly Challenges share three free starts each local day. A one-time Full Game purchase unlocks all 30 levels and unlimited starts, with no subscription.

Journey progress and records stay on your device. Game Center adds optional rankings, achievements and Play with Friends. TwinGlyph is available on Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 27 or later.
