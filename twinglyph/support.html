Support

Gameplay guidance, purchase restoration and direct support for TwinGlyph.

Start Here

TwinGlyph is a pair-matching game for iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro. Gameplay works offline once installed; purchases, purchase restoration and Game Center need their online services.

How to Play

On iPhone and iPad, tap a glyph to select it, then tap another with the same raised symbol and colour. Tap the first glyph again to deselect it. On Apple Vision Pro, look at a glyph and pinch to select it, then choose a matching glyph the same way.

Classic allows any remaining pair. From the Edge requires at least two empty neighbouring positions for a glyph to be playable. Dimmed glyphs become available as surrounding pairs are removed. Use Hint or Shuffle when available.

Fast consecutive matches increase your score multiplier up to ×5. A mismatch costs 10 points and adds two seconds. The Journey flow window gets shorter as you progress, but there is no time limit or game-over state.

Comfort

On iPhone and iPad, play in portrait or landscape. System Accessibility settings include Reduce Motion and larger text; the app follows these preferences.

On Apple Vision Pro, turn the Digital Crown to adjust immersion. To recenter, look toward the place where you want to play, then press and hold the Digital Crown. Settings can turn off Jade Sanctuary for passthrough play. Reduce Motion quiets scenery and effects.

Free Access and Full Game

Journey levels 1–5 remain free to replay. Free Play, Daily and Weekly Challenges share three free starts per local day; restarting these boards also uses a start. The one-time Full Game purchase unlocks all 30 Journey levels and removes the daily limit. There is no subscription.

Purchase or Restore Help

To recover an earlier purchase, open Settings → Restore Purchases, or choose Restore on the Full Game offer. Use the same Apple Account as the original purchase and connect to the internet.

If an offer fails to load, choose Retry Store after reconnecting. If a payment is awaiting approval, keep playing the free game while you wait and use Check Purchase Status later. Approved purchases unlock when Apple and RevenueCat return the updated access.

Apple handles billing and refunds through Report a Problem.

Progress and Rankings

Journey progress, one active Journey board and records are stored on this device. Free Play and shared challenges do not resume after relaunch. Deleting the app removes its local progress; restoring purchases only restores paid access.

Game Center is optional. If rankings are unavailable, check your Game Center sign-in in system Settings and internet connection. Daily boards use UTC dates; Weekly boards use ISO weeks. Pausing or leaving a shared challenge makes that attempt unranked. Play with Friends can invite others to the same Daily or Weekly board; Challenges may not appear in the Games app picker until Apple marks those catalog items Live.
