Privacy Policy

How TwinGlyph handles progress, purchases, spatial interaction and optional Apple services.

Overview

Effective September 7, 2026

TwinGlyph is developed by Mateusz Zając. It requires no TwinGlyph account, contains no advertising, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal data.

Data Stored on Your Device

Journey progress, one resumable Journey board, best records, achievement milestones, audio and environment preferences, and the free-game allowance are stored locally. TwinGlyph has no app-managed iCloud or cross-device progress synchronization. Removing the app removes its local app data; restoring a purchase does not restore Journey progress. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Game payment. TwinGlyph uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, maintain access and restore earlier purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information. TwinGlyph does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address or a custom account identifier, and the developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

Purchase information supports app functionality and purchase reporting. It is not used by TwinGlyph for advertising or cross-app tracking. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. When you sign in, TwinGlyph sends eligible scores and achievement progress to Apple and displays information supplied by Game Center, including rankings and player display names. These features follow your Apple Account and Game Center settings. You can play without Game Center. It does not back up your Journey progress.

Spatial Interaction

TwinGlyph uses visionOS and ARKit to place the board and respond to system recentering. Device pose is processed locally for placement. TwinGlyph does not upload head-pose samples, room geometry, eye-tracking data or hand-tracking recordings to the developer or RevenueCat. Gaze and pinch interactions are handled through the platform interaction APIs.

Diagnostics and Analytics

The public Release app writes diagnostic messages to the system log. It does not include a separate gameplay analytics or remote crash-reporting SDK. Apple may share aggregated usage and crash information according to your diagnostics-sharing settings. Internal test builds may keep additional on-device Pulse diagnostic logs; the public Release app disables those app-owned logging and console paths.

Support and This Website

If you contact support, your email address and information you choose to send are used to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private surroundings in screenshots. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion by email.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address, when serving the site. This website activity is separate from gameplay in the app.

Your Choices and Contact

You can use the game without Game Center, control Apple diagnostics sharing in system Settings, and remove local app data by deleting the app. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests about support correspondence, contact matteo.zajac@gmail.com. Apple and RevenueCat control the retention of information they process under their respective policies.
